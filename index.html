Dear Potential Partner,

Warm greetings from Pickle Cabsy Club!

This Christmas season, we're bringing our growing pickleball community together for DINKMAS 2026, a special year-end celebration on December 12, 2026. As one of the pioneering and biggest pickleball communities in Nueva Ecija, Pickle Cabsy Club has always been more than just a place to play. We are a community that brings people together through sports, friendship, healthy competition, and a shared passion for an active lifestyle.

As we celebrate another year of rallies, friendships, milestones, and memorable games, we would like to invite your company or business to become one of our valued event partners and sponsors. We are currently welcoming sponsorships and in-kind donations to help make our 3rd DINKMAS 2026 a memorable celebration for our members and guests.

Why Partner With Pickle Cabsy Club?

By supporting our community, you are:

Supporting Active & Healthy Lifestyles

Help encourage more people to discover sports, movement, fitness, and the benefits of an active lifestyle.

Investing in Community Building

Be part of a community that brings together players of different ages, backgrounds, professions, and skill levels through a shared passion for pickleball.

Growing Pickleball in Nueva Ecija

Help us continue introducing, developing, and making the sport more accessible to more people in our province.

Connecting Your Brand with a Growing Market

Gain exposure and build relationships with an engaged community of players, families, professionals, entrepreneurs, and local consumers.

Making a Difference Beyond the Court

Your support helps us create opportunities for connection, camaraderie, sportsmanship, and meaningful experiences.

In appreciation of your generosity, we will provide appropriate sponsor recognition through event announcements, social media acknowledgments, event collaterals, and other promotional opportunities, depending on the sponsorship arrangement.

We believe that great communities grow stronger when businesses and people come together.

Thank you for considering our invitation. Your support, in any form, will go a long way in making this celebration extra special.
